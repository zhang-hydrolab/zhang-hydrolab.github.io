Water Cycle and Ecohydrology水循环和生态水文

The team investigates how global warming and human activities are altering fundamental water cycle processes. They seek to reconcile different data sources (like satellite, model (such as PML-V2 evapotranspiration and HBV-PML hydrological models), and ground observations) to better quantify these changes, asking critical questions such as why parts of the water cycle appear to be stagnating despite warming, and how human actions reshape water availability and ecosystem functions—like the roles of forests as “sponges” or “pumps”—at regional and global scales.团队研究全球变暖和人类活动如何改变水循环的基本过程，融合卫星、模型（如 PML-V2 蒸散发模型与 HBV-PML 水文模型）和地面观测以更好地量化这些变化，并回答关键问题：为何升温背景下部分水循环过程反而趋于停滞，人类活动又如何在区域和全球尺度上重塑水资源可用性和生态系统功能，例如森林究竟扮演“海绵”还是“水泵”的角色。

PML_v2PML_v2 蒸散发产品

Penman-Monteith-Leuning Evapotranspiration V2 (PML_V2) products include evapotranspiration (ET), its three components, and gross primary production (GPP) at 500m and 8-day resolution during 2000-2023.Penman-Monteith-Leuning 蒸散发第二版（PML_V2）产品提供 2000—2023 年 500 m、8 天分辨率的蒸散发及其三个组分和总初级生产力（GPP）。

HBV-PMLHBV-PML 模型

The HBV-PML model, provides critical data support for global water resource assessments. This innovative model integrates the remote sensing evapotranspiration model PML with the classical hydrological model HBV, significantly improving the accuracy of evapotranspiration simulation through remote sensing observations. It effectively overcomes the limitations of traditional models in representing vegetation dynamics.HBV-PML 模型将遥感蒸散发模型 PML 与经典水文模型 HBV 相结合，利用遥感观测显著提高蒸散发模拟精度，克服了传统模型难以刻画植被动态的局限，为全球水资源评估提供关键数据支撑。

Water-Related Disasters and Security水灾害和水安全

This theme focuses on the mechanisms and impacts of extreme hydrological events, including droughts and floods. The group studies how droughts develop and propagate through different environmental layers, how flood patterns have evolved in China over decades, and how to enhance societal and ecological resilience to water-related extremes in a changing climate.这一方向聚焦干旱和洪水等极端水文事件的机理与影响，研究干旱如何在不同环境圈层中发展和传播，中国洪水格局在数十年间如何演变，以及如何在气候变化下增强社会和生态系统对水相关极端事件的韧性。

Fire Impacts and Cascading Hazards林火影响与级联灾害

This research examines how wildfires alter catchment water balance and intensify subsequent flood hazards. Studies in southeast Australia show that fire-induced vegetation and land-surface changes can modify evapotranspiration and streamflow, while amplifying flood peaks under certain hydroclimatic conditions. These findings highlight the cascading hydrological impacts of forest fires and their implications for flood risk.本研究关注林火如何改变流域水量平衡并加剧后续洪水灾害。在澳大利亚东南部的研究表明，林火引起的植被与陆面变化会改变蒸散发和径流，并在特定水文气候条件下放大洪峰。这些发现揭示了林火的级联水文影响及其对洪水风险的意义。

Drought Recovery and Hydrological Resilience干旱恢复与水文韧性

This research investigates how large river basins recover from severe droughts and why recovery varies across regions. By integrating GRACE satellite observations with global water availability estimates, we quantify terrestrial water storage recovery and identify pronounced disparities between humid and dryland basins. We further examine how post-drought water availability, recurrent droughts, and human water use influence recovery trajectories and long-term hydrological resilience.本研究关注严重干旱后大型流域水文系统的恢复过程及其区域差异。通过融合 GRACE 卫星观测与全球水资源可利用量估算，量化陆地水储量恢复过程，揭示湿润与干旱流域之间显著的恢复差异，并进一步分析干旱后水分可利用量、重复干旱和人类用水对水文恢复及长期韧性的影响。

Innovating Water Science水文水资源与新技术

The group leverages advanced technologies such as high-resolution remote sensing and artificial intelligence (AI) to improve the monitoring and prediction of hydrological processes. They develop fine-scale datasets for evapotranspiration and vegetation productivity, integrate AI with physical models to enable better forecasting in ungauged basins, and work toward real-time flood prediction systems that support water resources management under climate change.团队利用高分辨率遥感和人工智能等先进技术改进水文过程的监测与预测：研发蒸散发和植被生产力的精细尺度数据集，融合人工智能与物理模型以提升无资料流域的预报能力，并致力于发展实时洪水预报系统，为气候变化下的水资源管理提供支撑。

AI for Hydrology Modeling人工智能水文建模

We introduce an AI-driven Transformer-based deep learning model to address data gaps in GRACE and GRACE Follow-On satellite data. By integrating meteorological and hydrological features like temperature, precipitation, and evapotranspiration, the model effectively fills the 11-month data gap, showing superior accuracy, particularly in arid regions. The transformer-based approach outperforms traditional methods, demonstrating the potential of AI in enhancing satellite data continuity and reliability for environmental monitoring.我们提出基于 Transformer 的人工智能深度学习模型，用于填补 GRACE 与 GRACE Follow-On 卫星观测之间的数据缺口。模型融合气温、降水和蒸散发等气象水文要素，有效填补约 11 个月的观测空缺，精度优于传统方法，在干旱区表现尤为突出，展示了人工智能在提升卫星数据连续性与可靠性方面的潜力。

CAS-Canglong中科苍龙

We present a novel AI-based approach for sub-seasonal to seasonal (S2S) global sea surface temperature (SST) prediction, using a 3D Transformer model called CAS-Canglong. By leveraging the self-attention mechanism inherent in Transformer architectures, the model efficiently handles the complexities of ocean-atmosphere interactions and improves prediction accuracy. Compared to traditional physics-based models, CAS-Canglong demonstrates superior skill in predicting SST patterns, enhancing computational efficiency and forecasting accuracy for up to three months in advance. This approach shows great potential for improving predictions of extreme weather events like droughts and floods, as well as for future climate studies.我们提出面向全球海表温度次季节至季节（S2S）预测的人工智能方法——三维 Transformer 模型“中科苍龙”（CAS-Canglong）。模型利用自注意力机制刻画复杂的海气相互作用，与传统物理模式相比，在最长三个月的提前期内具有更高的海温预测技巧和计算效率，可为干旱、洪水等极端事件预报和未来气候研究提供支撑。
