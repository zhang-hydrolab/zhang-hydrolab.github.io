Data products数据产品

Gridded evapotranspiration, gross primary production and terrestrial water storage products, free to download.免费开放的蒸散发、总初级生产力和陆地水储量格点数据产品。

Temporal coverage时间覆盖

PML_v2.2

Coupled ET and GPP extending PML_v2 back to 1982 by integrating MODIS and AVHRR archives; calibrated at 208 flux stations.融合 MODIS 与 AVHRR 存档，将 PML_v2 的耦合蒸散发和总初级生产力数据向前延伸至 1982 年，并基于 208 个通量站点率定。

Scope范围Global
Variables变量ET, GPP
Time step时间分辨率8-day · half-month
Grid空间分辨率500 m · 0.1°
Period时段1982 – near present

Download下载

  • 500 m · Google Earth Engine
  • 0.1° · National Tibetan Plateau Data Center

How to cite引用方式

  1. Xu, Z., Zhang, Y., Kong, D., Ma, N., & Zhang, X. (2026). Extended global terrestrial evapotranspiration and gross primary production dataset from 1982 to near present. Earth System Science Data Discussions. https://essd.copernicus.org/preprints/essd-2026-94/

PML_v2

Penman-Monteith-Leuning Evapotranspiration V2: evapotranspiration, its three components, and gross primary production.Penman-Monteith-Leuning 蒸散发第二版产品，包含蒸散发及其三个分量和总初级生产力。

Scope范围Global
Variables变量ET, ET components, GPP
Time step时间分辨率8-day
Grid空间分辨率500 m · 0.05°
Period时段2002.07 – near present

Download下载

  • 500 m · Google Earth Engine
  • 0.05° · National Tibetan Plateau Data Center

How to cite引用方式

  1. Zhang, Y., Peña-Arancibia, J.L., McVicar, T.R., Chiew, F.H.S., Vaze, J., Liu, C., Lu, X., Zheng, H., Wang, Y., Liu, Y.Y., Miralles, D.G., Pan, M., 2016. Multi-decadal trends in global terrestrial evapotranspiration and its components. Sci. Rep. 6, 19124. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep19124.

  2. Zhang, Y., Kong, D., Gan, R., Chiew, F.H.S., McVicar, T.R., Zhang, Q., & Yang, Y. (2019). Coupled estimation of 500m and 8-day resolution global evapotranspiration and gross primary production in 2002-2017. Remote Sensing Environ. 222, 165-182. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2018.12.031.

PML_v2-China

Daily, 500 m coupled evapotranspiration and gross primary production across China.中国区域逐日、500 米分辨率的耦合蒸散发和总初级生产力数据集。

Scope范围China
Variables变量ET, GPP
Time step时间分辨率Daily
Grid空间分辨率500 m
Period时段2000.02.26 – 2020.12.31

Download下载

  • National Tibetan Plateau Data Center

How to cite引用方式

  1. He, S., Zhang, Y., Ma, N., Tian, J., Kong, D., & Liu, C. (2022). A daily and 500 m coupled evapotranspiration and gross primary production product across China during 2000–2020, Earth Syst. Sci. Data., https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-2022-183.

GRACE-Transformer-Gap

Global gap filling of GRACE CSR RL06 terrestrial water storage using a Transformer-based deep learning model.基于 Transformer 深度学习模型对 GRACE CSR RL06 陆地水储量进行全球空缺填补。

Scope范围Global
Variables变量Terrestrial water storage
Time step时间分辨率Monthly
Grid空间分辨率0.25°
Period时段2002 – near present
