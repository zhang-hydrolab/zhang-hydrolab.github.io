    Professor of Hydrology, TU Wien维也纳技术大学水文学教授

    TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology)奥地利维也纳技术大学 · Vienna维也纳

    TU Wien: Stockholm Water Prize

    Günter Blöschl is Professor and Head of the Institute of Hydraulic Engineering and Water Resources Management at TU Wien, where he also directs the Centre for Water Resource Systems and chairs the Vienna Doctoral Programme on Water Resource Systems. He studied civil engineering at TU Wien and has worked at the University of British Columbia, the University of Melbourne and the Australian National University. Known for his work on floods and socio-hydrology, he received the 2025 Stockholm Water Prize, an ERC Advanced Grant and the AGU Robert E. Horton Medal, and is a member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences and the US National Academy of Engineering.

    Günter Blöschl 是维也纳技术大学水利工程与水资源管理研究所所长、教授，同时担任水资源系统中心主任和维也纳水资源系统博士项目负责人。他毕业于维也纳技术大学土木工程专业，曾在加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚大学、澳大利亚墨尔本大学和澳大利亚国立大学从事研究。他以洪水水文与社会水文学研究著称，获 2025 年斯德哥尔摩水奖、欧洲研究理事会高级资助和美国地球物理联合会 Robert E. Horton 奖章，是奥地利科学院院士和美国国家工程院外籍院士。

  • Thorsten Wagener

    Alexander von Humboldt Professor, University of Potsdam德国波茨坦大学洪堡教授

    University of Potsdam德国波茨坦大学 · Potsdam波茨坦

    University of Potsdam profile

    Thorsten Wagener has held an Alexander von Humboldt Professorship at the University of Potsdam since 2021. He studied civil engineering at the University of Siegen and TU Delft, received his PhD from Imperial College London, worked at Pennsylvania State University, and led water and environmental engineering at the University of Bristol from 2012 to 2020. His research covers catchment hydrology, catchment classification, prediction in ungauged basins and the evaluation of environmental models. He is a Fellow of the European Academy of Sciences, the Royal Academy of Engineering and the American Geophysical Union, and received the EGU John Dalton Medal in 2026.

    Thorsten Wagener 自 2021 年起任德国波茨坦大学亚历山大·冯·洪堡教授。他先后在锡根大学和代尔夫特理工大学学习土木工程，在伦敦帝国理工学院获博士学位，曾任职于美国宾夕法尼亚州立大学，并于 2012—2020 年领导英国布里斯托大学水与环境工程方向。他的研究涉及流域水文、流域分类、无资料流域预测和环境模型评估，是欧洲科学院院士、英国皇家工程院院士和美国地球物理联合会会士，2026 年获欧洲地球科学联合会 John Dalton 奖章。

  • Diego Miralles

    Professor of Hydrology and Climate, Ghent University比利时根特大学水文与气候教授

    Ghent University比利时根特大学 · Ghent根特

    Homepage

    Diego Miralles has been Professor in Hydrology and Climate at Ghent University since 2017, after positions in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and the United States, and leads the Hydro-Climate Extremes Lab (H-CEL). His research spans ecohydrology, land–atmosphere interactions, global hydrology, hydro-climatic extremes and satellite remote sensing. He has received ERC Starting and Consolidator Grants and is a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher.

    Diego Miralles 自 2017 年起任比利时根特大学水文与气候教授，主持水文气候极端事件实验室（H-CEL），此前曾在英国、荷兰和美国从事研究。他的研究涵盖生态水文、陆气相互作用、全球水文、水文气候极端事件和卫星遥感，曾获欧洲研究理事会启动资助和巩固资助，并入选科睿唯安全球高被引科学家。

  • Ruby Leung

    Battelle Fellow, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory美国太平洋西北国家实验室 Battelle Fellow

    Pacific Northwest National Laboratory美国太平洋西北国家实验室 · Richland, WA里奇兰

    PNNL: National Academy of Engineering

    L. Ruby Leung is a Battelle Fellow at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory and Chief Scientist of the Energy Exascale Earth System Model (E3SM). Her research spans Earth system modelling, mountain hydrometeorology, aerosol–cloud interactions, orographic precipitation and climate extremes. She was elected to the US National Academy of Engineering in 2017, was named a DOE Distinguished Scientist Fellow in 2021, and is a Fellow of AGU, AMS and AAAS. She holds a BS from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and an MS and PhD in atmospheric sciences from Texas A&M University.

    Ruby Leung 是美国太平洋西北国家实验室 Battelle Fellow，并担任能源部百亿亿次地球系统模式（E3SM）首席科学家。她的研究涵盖地球系统模拟、山地水文气象、气溶胶—云相互作用、地形降水和气候极端事件。她于 2017 年当选美国国家工程院院士，2021 年获美国能源部杰出科学家称号，是美国地球物理联合会、美国气象学会和美国科学促进会会士。她在香港中文大学获学士学位，在得克萨斯农工大学获大气科学硕士和博士学位。

  • Francis Chiew

    Senior Principal Research Scientist, CSIRO澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织高级首席研究科学家

    CSIRO澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织（CSIRO） · Canberra堪培拉

    Homepage

    Francis Chiew leads the Surface Water and Basin Outcomes group at CSIRO in Canberra, which he joined in 2006 after 15 years at the University of Melbourne, where he received his PhD in hydrology in 1991. His work covers water resources assessment, hydrological forecasting and prediction, climate change impact and adaptation, and integrated basin management; he was among the first to quantify the link between the El Niño–Southern Oscillation and global streamflow. He received the 2022 Volker Medal (International Hydrology Prize) and was a Lead Author of the IPCC Fifth and Sixth Assessment Reports.

    Francis Chiew 现任澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织（CSIRO）地表水与流域成效研究组负责人。他 1991 年在墨尔本大学获水文学博士学位，在该校工作 15 年后于 2006 年加入 CSIRO。他的研究涉及水资源评估、水文预报与预测、气候变化影响与适应以及流域综合管理，是最早量化厄尔尼诺—南方涛动与全球径流关系的学者之一。他获 2022 年国际水文奖 Volker 奖章，并担任 IPCC 第五次和第六次评估报告主要作者。

  • Tim McVicar

    Research Scientist, CSIRO Environment澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织环境部研究科学家

    CSIRO澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织（CSIRO） · Canberra堪培拉

    Homepage

    Tim McVicar is a Research Scientist who leads the Biophysical Dynamics Team in the Living Landscapes Program of CSIRO Environment. With over 35 years of experience in ecohydrology, climatology, remote sensing, and spatial interpolation, he develops high-resolution estimates of actual evapotranspiration across Australia using satellite imagery. His research focuses on modelling AET, monitoring vegetation dynamics, assessing drought and climate variability, and understanding surface-energy exchanges through satellite–meteorological data integration.

    Tim McVicar 是澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织（CSIRO）环境部研究科学家，领导“生命景观”项目中的生物物理动态团队。他在生态水文、气候学、遥感和空间插值领域拥有 35 年以上研究经验，利用卫星影像发展澳大利亚高分辨率实际蒸散发估算，研究方向包括实际蒸散发模拟、植被动态监测、干旱与气候变率评估，以及基于卫星与气象数据融合的地表能量交换。

    Amir AghaKouchak is a Chancellor’s Professor of Civil and Environmental Engineering and Director of the Center for Hydrometeorology and Remote Sensing (CHRS) at the University of California, Irvine. His research bridges hydrology, climatology and remote sensing, focusing on natural hazards, climate extremes, and compound and cascading events. He has authored over 270 peer-reviewed papers and is a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher. His honours include the AGU James B. Macelwane Medal, the EGU Plinius Medal (2026), the ASCE Norman Medal and Huber Research Prize, and the AMS Robert E. Horton Lectureship in Hydrology, and he is a Fellow of AGU, ASCE and EWRI.
